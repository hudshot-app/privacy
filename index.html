Privacy Policy — HUDshot

Effective date: 19 September 2026 Last updated: 2 October 2026

HUDshot is a dashcam and ride-recording app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone.

The short version: HUDshot does not collect your data. The app itself never sends anything it records off your device. The only exception is an optional diagnostic report, which is sent only if you choose to send it yourself (see section 5).

1. Who is responsible

HUDshot is developed and published by Saquib Hussain, an individual developer based in India.

Contact: hudshot.support@gmail.com

2. What HUDshot does not do

HUDshot does not:

  • send your videos, location, or any other information to me or to anyone else, unless you choose to send a diagnostic report (section 5)
  • contain advertising, or any advertising identifier
  • contain analytics, tracking software, or any automatic crash reporting
  • require an account, a sign-in, or any personal detail
  • share or sell information to third parties

I do not operate a server that receives your recordings, and I have no ability to access anything stored on your phone.

3. What the app uses, and why

HUDshot asks for the following permissions. Each is used only on your device, while you are using the app.

Permission Why it is needed
Camera To record video.
Microphone To record the audio track of that video.
Location (precise and approximate) To show and record speed, altitude, distance, heading and position. These readings are drawn onto the screen while you ride and onto the video you export.
Notifications To show the progress notification while a video is being processed.
Foreground service To keep processing running if you switch away from the app mid-export.
Network access Declared for features that are not enabled in this version. See section 7.

Location is used only while the app is open and in use. HUDshot does not request background location access and cannot track you when the app is not running.

4. What is stored on your phone

Video recordings. Recorded video and exported video are saved to your device's normal video storage, where your gallery app can see them. They stay on your phone and are yours to keep, move or delete. HUDshot itself never uploads them. Other apps you use, such as a cloud photo backup, may upload videos from your gallery under their own settings.

After a video is successfully processed, the app deletes the original unprocessed recording from your phone, keeping the finished version. This happens automatically on your device. No copy is sent anywhere.

What your exported video shows. Speed, altitude, heading, distance, the date and time, and, in some themes, the destination name you typed and your chosen marker photo are drawn into the picture of the exported video. If you share that video, anyone who watches it can see this information. HUDshot does not add your GPS coordinates to the video file's hidden metadata.

Ride data. Speed, location, altitude, heading and similar readings are used while the video is made and are drawn into the picture. They are not stored in a separate file or database.

Your destination name. If you type a destination in the Destiny theme, HUDshot saves it on your phone so it is still there next time you open the app. It is kept in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall HUDshot.

Your chosen marker photo. If you pick a picture from your gallery to use as a map marker, HUDshot copies it into the app's own private storage, scaled down and cropped. Only one picture is kept, and choosing a new one replaces it. Other apps cannot read it. It is removed when you uninstall HUDshot.

This version has no button to delete that picture from within the app. Uninstalling HUDshot removes it. A way to clear it inside the app is planned.

Diagnostic log. A small technical log is kept in the app's private storage. See section 5.

5. Diagnostic reports (optional)

HUDshot keeps a small technical log on your phone to help fix problems. It records HUDshot's own events (recordings, video exports, errors, crashes and button taps) and basic phone details: model, Android version, app version, free storage, and whether each permission is granted. It does not contain your location, your videos, account details or device identifiers.

The log stays in the app's private storage, is limited in size (older entries are removed automatically), and is deleted when you uninstall HUDshot.

It never leaves your phone automatically. It is shared only if you tap Settings → Send diagnostic report, read what it contains, and choose an app (for example email or WhatsApp) to send it with. Reports are used only to find and fix problems in HUDshot and are deleted once the problem is resolved.

6. Children

HUDshot is not directed at children and is not designed for use by them.

7. Internet access

HUDshot declares network permission because later versions are intended to offer optional features that use the internet, such as weather and route information.

In this version those features are switched off, and the app makes no network requests. If a future version turns them on, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the update will say exactly what is sent and to whom.
